My story

I'm Priyanshu Sharma, a 14-year-old student. I was born in Rajasthan, and my family roots are in Shahpura, in the Jaipur district. Today I live in Delhi, where I study and prepare for the future I want to build.

I love learning. When something new catches my attention, like a new AI tool, a math idea or a science concept, I want to understand how it works. I spend a lot of time learning about AI and practising prompting, which means learning to ask the right question so AI gives a useful answer.

Math and science are my favourite subjects, and cricket is how I play and relax. My biggest dream is to become a businessman, build companies that help people, and one day be on the list of the world's richest people.
